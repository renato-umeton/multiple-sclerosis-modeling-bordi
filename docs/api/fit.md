# fit

The statistics the article does not report. It states two mean durations and
two qualitative claims, that the durations carry no typical scale and that the
relapses carry no typical period, and it reports no fit, no test and no
interval anywhere. This module supplies them, so that the claims can be checked
rather than repeated: duration fits with and without the censoring of a final
remission, four tests of memorylessness, negative binomial count fits against a
Poisson comparison, gamma fits of the per patient rates, a periodicity test
over relapse onsets, and equation (7) applied to observed durations.

Every result object carries the citation of the article on a `citation`
property.
