# model

The potential of equations (2) and (3) and everything that follows from it
alone: the two wells and the barrier between them, the fold at which the
shallower well disappears, the mean exit times of equations (5) and (6) with
and without the Kramers prefactor, the exact mean first passage time of the
stochastic equation, the barrier ratio of equation (7), and the calibration
that turns a pair of observed episode durations into a pair of model
parameters.

Nothing here draws a random number. The [theory page](../theory.md) derives
each of these in the order the article introduces them, and says where a
reading had to be chosen because the article leaves it open.
