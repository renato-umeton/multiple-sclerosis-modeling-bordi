# edss

An illustrative EDSS trajectory driven by the weekly relapse series, with every
parameter drawn from the literature that followed the article rather than from
the article itself, which reports no disability score of any kind.
[Disability trajectory](../disability.md) sets out the model, the evidence
behind each parameter, the caveats and the references, and nothing here is fit
for prognosis or for clinical use.
